Hall Monitor Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android game Hall Monitor (the "app"), published by GoesRound ("I", "me", "my"), handles information. The short version: I don't run any servers for the app, I don't have accounts, and I don't collect your personal information myself. The app shows ads and sells optional in-app purchases through Google, and those Google services handle some data, as described below.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email prosperous.abundant.plentiful@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (such as your round, cash, upgrades and settings) in files on your own device. This information stays on your device. I do not receive a copy and I cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, including optional ads you can choose to watch for rewards. To serve ads, AdMob (Google) may collect and use information from your device, which can include your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and software information, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics such as crash logs. This information is collected and used by Google, not by me, and Google may share it with its advertising partners.

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form before ads are loaded, and you can choose whether to allow personalised ads.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details and purchase records; I never see or store your card or payment information. The app stores a record of purchase receipts on your device so that purchases are delivered once and restored correctly. Google's handling of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the "max offline alert" setting and allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your device to tell you when your offline earnings are full. This notification is created on your device. It does not involve any server and no data is sent anywhere for it. You can turn it off in the app's settings or in Android's notification settings.

Information I do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-in, and no name, email address or phone number is collected by the app.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools of my own.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, files or location.

Sharing

I do not sell your personal information and I do not receive any of it from the app. The only sharing of data is the data Google AdMob and Google Play collect directly, as described above.

Data security

Your game progress stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Ad and purchase data is transmitted to Google over encrypted connections and is handled under Google's security practices.

Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device: kept until you delete it. Uninstalling the app removes it, and you can also use "Reset all progress" in the app's settings.
  • Data held by Google (ads and purchases): retained and deleted under Google's policies. You can manage or delete your Google activity data at myactivity.google.com.
  • Because I don't collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for me to delete on my side. If you believe I do hold information about you, email me and we can chat.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and think a child has provided information, please contact me and I will help.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data (such as access, deletion or objection). Since I do not hold personal data about you, these requests mostly apply to Google; you can use Google's tools linked above. You can also contact me at the email address above.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
